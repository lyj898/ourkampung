About OurKampung

Who writes these guides, and how we handle running businesses in the same space.

Who we are

OurKampung is written by the team behind Junk to Clear, a Singapore disposal and clearance business established in 2009, and is the home of its family of sister sites. The service sites cover moving, cleaning, pest control, aircon and handyman jobs: enquiries go to the team behind Junk to Clear, and partner firms do the work. The other guide sites cover property you don't live in, and moving abroad. See all our sites.

Kampung means village. The name is about the kind of advice you'd once have got from a neighbour who'd done it all before: practical, specific, and honest about what's worth paying for and what isn't.

How we write

  • Useful on its ownEvery guide should help whether or not you hire anyone. Plenty of it is DIY, and we say so.
  • Specific to SingaporeHDB rules, condo house rules, tenancy agreements and the climate — not advice written for somewhere else.
  • Sourced where it mattersFor rules and deadlines we link to the agency responsible. Rules change, so check the source before you rely on it.
  • Honest about what variesTenancy agreements, condo rules and renovation contracts differ. Where it depends on yours, we say so.

When we link to our own sites

Inside a guide, only where it covers something one of our sites actually does — a move-out clean in the tenancy guide, aircon servicing in the aircon guide. Most of those links sit in a clearly labelled "Need a hand with this?" box at the end of each guide. The footer and our sites page list every site in the family. We don't rank our own sites against competitors, and we don't publish "best of" lists.

Where one of our sister sites has already written a good guide, we link to it rather than write a competing one.

Corrections and suggestions

If something in a guide is out of date or wrong, or there's a topic you'd like covered, please tell us. We update guides when rules or practices change, and every guide shows when it was last updated. For bookings and quotes, use the service site for the job; our sites page says which.
