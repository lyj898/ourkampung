Bluey Theme

Bluey-inspired parties, backyard fun, beautifully styled.

Inspired by everyone's favourite blue heeler and her backyard games, we style Bluey-themed parties in playful blue and burnt-orange tones, with a relaxed 'backyard play' feel that suits toddlers and their older siblings alike. Think Keepy Uppy corners and a grassy-green base rather than an exact reproduction — cheerful, recognisable and genuinely well styled. We coordinate the whole thing, from balloon backdrop to cake.

Styling Ideas

Ways we style a bluey party

  • A blue-and-orange balloon backdrop with a playful backyard-game feel
  • A 'Keepy Uppy' balloon-tap zone and simple backyard-style party games
  • Puppy-ear headbands for every little guest
  • A bone-shaped or paw-print cake in cheerful blue and orange

Good To Know

Bluey party FAQs

Do you use official Bluey characters or artwork?
We style parties inspired by the show's colours, games and backyard-play spirit rather than using licensed artwork, which keeps the look tasteful and fully bespoke to your child — we're not affiliated with or endorsed by the show.
What age is a Bluey party best for?
It's most loved by toddlers and preschoolers, roughly ages two to five, and we keep the games gentle and easy to join in with at that age.
Can you include backyard-style games even indoors?
Yes — simple games like a balloon-tap 'Keepy Uppy' corner work well indoors in a function room or living room, no actual backyard required.
