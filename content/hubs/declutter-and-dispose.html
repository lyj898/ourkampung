Clearing out is easier when you know where things go. Much of what's in a typical home has a proper route out: electronics and batteries go to e-waste collection, working items can be sold or donated, and bulky items such as furniture and mattresses need a booked collection or a disposal service.

We've started with e-waste, because it's the category people are least sure about and the one that matters most to get right — a battery in the refuse chute is a fire risk, and a phone recycled without being wiped still holds your data.

For furniture, mattresses and general decluttering, our sister site JunkToClear has already written the guides, so rather than repeat them here, we've linked them below.
