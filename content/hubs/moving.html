Most moves in Singapore go smoothly when the few things with long lead times are booked early — movers at peak periods, condo lift bookings and your internet line — and go wrong when they're left to the last fortnight. Everything else is packing and patience.

Start with the week-by-week checklist, which covers the whole move, from eight weeks out to the one legal deadline afterwards. If either home is a condo, read the move-day rules too: condos usually need a lift booking, a deposit and a set time window, and discovering that a few days beforehand is the most common avoidable problem.
