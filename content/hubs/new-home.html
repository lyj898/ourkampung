Moving into a new home — a BTO, a resale flat you've renovated, a condo you've just bought — involves more steps than the move itself, and the order matters. Inspect first, so defects are recorded before renovation covers them. Renovate second. Clean third, once the dust has genuinely settled. Move in last.

The BTO guide walks through the whole sequence from key collection, including what to check on inspection day and how the defects liability period works. The post-renovation checklist is for any home that's just been renovated: the defects to find before you make the final payment, what to do with leftover debris, and what a proper post-renovation clean covers.
