Dinosaur Theme

Dinosaur parties, prehistoric fun, beautifully done.

Dinosaur parties are pure joy for little explorers — and in our hands they look as good as they are fun. We lean into earthy greens, lush foliage and playful dino details rather than garish plastic, so the setup is adventurous for the kids and easy on the eye for you. Our Singapore team handles the lot, start to finish.

Styling Ideas

Ways we style a dinosaur party

  • A jungle foliage balloon installation with friendly dinosaur figures
  • A fossil-dig sensory pit and dino-egg treasure hunt for the guests
  • A volcano or dino-egg cake with cracked-shell detailing
  • Explorer party favours — magnifying glasses and mini dino sets

Good To Know

Dinosaur party FAQs

What ages is a dinosaur party best for?
It's a hit from toddlers right up to older kids. We scale the activities — sensory fossil digs for little ones, treasure hunts and games for the bigger explorers.
Can you include entertainment like a dinosaur mascot?
Yes. We can arrange a walkabout dinosaur mascot, a host to run the games, or an interactive science-style show, depending on your budget and space.
Do you set up outdoors, like at a park or garden?
We do — outdoor dino parties look wonderful. We'll advise on weather-safe setups and bring shade or a wet-weather plan where needed.
