Home/Ending a tenancy

Hand back the keys, keep your deposit.

What your tenancy agreement usually asks of you at the end, what landlords commonly deduct for, and how to prove the flat's condition.

Where to start

  1. Read your agreement's handover clauses. Landlords deduct for what the agreement makes your responsibility, measured against the flat's condition when you moved in. The handover checklist covers the clauses to check and fair wear and tear.
  2. Fix what's yours to fix. The handyman fixes guide covers the small repairs an inspection usually picks up, and which to leave to a professional.
  3. Clean, then photograph every room on handover day. Most deposit disputes come down to missing evidence.
