Home/Ending a tenancy

Hand back the keys, keep your deposit.

What your tenancy agreement usually asks of you at the end, what landlords commonly deduct for, and how to prove the flat's condition.

Getting your deposit back in full is mostly a matter of preparation. Landlords deduct for what the tenancy agreement makes your responsibility, measured against the flat's condition when you moved in — so the tenants who get their deposits back are the ones who read those clauses, fixed and cleaned what they needed to, and photographed everything on the way out.

Start with the handover checklist, which covers the clauses to check, the difference between fair wear and tear and damage, and what to do on handover day. Then work through the handyman fixes worth doing first — the small repairs an inspection usually picks up, and which ones to leave to a professional.
