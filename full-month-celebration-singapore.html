Full Month

Full month celebrations, warm and beautifully done.

The full-month celebration is a treasured Singaporean tradition — a first gathering to introduce your baby to family and friends. We style it with a soft, elegant touch and coordinate the details that matter, from the welcome table to the guest gifts, so you can be present with your loved ones rather than running the show.

The Detail

What we take care of

  • A gentle, elegant setup in tones that suit your baby and venue
  • Coordination of catering and a celebratory cake or dessert table
  • Help arranging traditional guest gifts and red-egg touches if you'd like
  • Full delivery, setup and teardown, at home or a function room
Good To Know

Full Month FAQs

Can you include traditional full-month elements?
Yes — we can weave in the customary touches such as guest gift boxes and red-egg-and-ginger elements alongside a modern, tasteful setup. Tell us which traditions matter to your family.
Do you cater for full-month gatherings at home?
We do. Homes and condo function rooms are perfect for full-month celebrations, and we handle delivery, setup and clearing so the space is left as we found it.
How far ahead should we book?
Two to three weeks gives us the most flexibility, but we can often help on shorter notice — message us with your date and we'll do our best.
