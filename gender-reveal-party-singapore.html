Gender Reveal

Gender reveals, joyful and picture-perfect.

A gender reveal is all about one joyful moment — and we make sure it lands beautifully. We style a fresh, photogenic setup, coordinate the reveal itself (kept a secret from you, if you like), and handle the details so the surprise goes off without a hitch and the photos are wonderful.

The Detail

What we take care of

  • A bright, photogenic setup ready for the big moment
  • A coordinated reveal — cake, balloon, or another tasteful option
  • The secret kept safe between us and your helper
  • Delivery, setup and teardown all taken care of
Good To Know

Gender Reveal FAQs

How can you keep the reveal a surprise from us?
We coordinate directly with your clinic or a trusted helper so neither parent sees the result — only the reveal moment does the telling.
What reveal options do you offer?
Popular choices include a cut-to-reveal cake, a colour-filled balloon, or a confetti moment. We'll suggest what suits your space and guest list.
Can we combine it with a baby shower?
Definitely — many families do both in one gathering. We'll plan a flow that fits both moments comfortably.
