How It Works

From first message to happy ending.

Planning a party should feel exciting, not exhausting. Here's how we make the whole thing effortless — with one team and one point of contact throughout.

01

Share your vision

Tell us the occasion, the age, rough guest count and any ideas you have — through our quick planner or a WhatsApp message. No detail is too small, and nothing is set in stone.

02

Get a tailored plan

Your dedicated concierge curates a complete party proposal — theme, styling, entertainment, cake, catering and venue — matched to your vision and budget comfort. Usually within one working day.

03

Refine & confirm

Tweak anything you like over chat until it's just right. Once you're happy, we lock in every element and coordinate all the moving parts behind the scenes — you don't lift a finger.

04

Enjoy the day

Our team handles delivery, set-up, hosting and pack-down. You get to be fully present — celebrating with your child and your guests, camera in hand, worry-free.

Good To Know

Common questions

How far in advance should I plan?

We recommend reaching out 3–4 weeks ahead for the widest choice, but we regularly arrange beautiful parties on shorter notice. If your date is soon, message us on WhatsApp and we'll do our best.

How much does a party cost?

Every party is tailored, so we prepare a personalised quote based on your guest count, services and budget comfort. Share your details through the planner and we'll send a clear proposal — with no obligation.

Can I book just one service?

Absolutely. Need only a cake, or just décor? We're happy to help with a single element or the entire celebration — whatever makes your life easier.

Do you have a minimum spend?

We work with a range of budgets. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll craft the best possible experience within it.

Which areas do you cover?

We operate islandwide across Singapore — HDBs, condos, landed homes, restaurants, clubhouses and indoor play venues.
