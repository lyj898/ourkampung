How Much Does a Kids' Birthday Party Cost in Singapore? (2026 Guide)

One of the first questions every parent asks — and the honest answer is 'it depends'. Here's a realistic breakdown for Singapore in 2026, so you can budget with confidence.

3 Aug 2026 · 6 min read

The short answer

Most styled children's birthday parties in Singapore land somewhere between $300 and $2,500, depending on guest numbers, venue and how much you'd like done for you. A simple at-home celebration with balloons and a cake can cost a few hundred dollars; a fully styled party with décor, entertainment, catering and photography sits higher. The good news: you can dial the spend up or down at almost every step.

What actually drives the cost

Four things move the number more than anything else:

  • Guest count — more children means more catering, more party favours and often a bigger space.
  • Venue — a party at home or a condo function room is far cheaper than a restaurant or dedicated venue.
  • Décor — balloon styling ranges from a simple garland to a full backdrop installation.
  • Extras — entertainment, a custom cake, catering and photography each add to the total but also lift the day.

Three sample budgets

Cosy (from ~$300–$600): at home or a function room, a balloon garland, a lovely cake, and a simple grazing spread you top up yourself.

Mid-range (~$700–$1,500): a styled backdrop, custom cake and dessert table, light catering, and one entertainment act such as a magician or face painter.

Premium (~$1,500–$2,500+): a full styled setup, catering for adults and children, a headline entertainer, and a photographer to capture it all.

How to save without losing the magic

  • Choose a condo function room — often the single biggest saving.
  • Invest in one statement element (usually the backdrop) rather than spreading thin across many.
  • Keep the guest list tight for younger children — toddlers do beautifully in small groups.
  • Let one concierge coordinate everything, so you're not paying for overlaps or last-minute premiums.

Good To Know

Common questions

Is it cheaper to have a party at home?
Usually, yes — a home or condo function room removes venue-hire costs, which is often the biggest single expense. Our styling makes the space feel special without the price tag of a dedicated venue.
How much should I budget per child?
As a rough guide, catering and favours run around $15-$40 per child depending on the spread. Décor and entertainment are mostly fixed costs that don't scale much with headcount.
