Who writes OurKampung

OurKampung is written by the team behind four Singapore home-services businesses: HomeToMoved for moving, HomeToClean for cleaning, JunkToClear for disposal, and SkillsToFix for handyman repairs, aircon servicing and pest control. These guides draw on the work those businesses do in Singapore homes.

That gives us an interest, so here's how we handle it: every guide is written to be useful whether or not you ever hire anyone, and we only link to our businesses where a guide covers something they actually do. More about how we work.
