Cakes & Desserts

Birthday cakes, as good as they look.

The cake is the moment everyone gathers for. We work with trusted bakers to arrange custom cakes and dessert tables that match your theme and taste as good as they look — no dry, over-iced letdowns. From a single statement cake to a full dessert spread, we coordinate flavours, design and delivery so it arrives perfect and on time.

The Detail

What's included

  • Custom cakes designed around your theme and colours
  • Matching dessert tables — cupcakes, cookies, cake pops and more
  • Trusted bakers, with flavours and dietary needs accounted for
  • Coordinated delivery timed to your party
Good To Know

Birthday Cakes FAQs

Can you accommodate dietary needs like halal or nut-free?
Yes — tell us your requirements and we'll match you with a baker who caters for them, whether that's halal, eggless, nut-free or other needs.
Do you offer a cake plus a smash cake?
We can arrange both — a main cake for guests and a separate smash cake for first-birthday photos.
How far ahead do cakes need to be ordered?
One to two weeks is ideal for custom designs, though we can sometimes arrange quicker. Let us know your date.
