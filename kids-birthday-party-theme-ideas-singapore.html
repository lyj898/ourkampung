Kids' Birthday Party Theme Ideas for 2026 (Singapore)

The theme sets the tone for everything else. Here are our favourites for 2026 — each one we style with a refined, photogenic twist rather than the usual plastic clutter.

3 Aug 2026 · 5 min read

For toddlers (1–3)

Little ones respond to soft colour and gentle play. Our most-loved toddler themes:

  • Unicorn — dreamy pastels and a little sparkle.
  • Pastel rainbow — joyful and gender-neutral.
  • Cocomelon — bright, cheery and instantly recognisable.
  • Safari & jungle — perfect for a 'Wild One' first birthday.

For younger kids (3–6)

  • Rescue pups — adventure and mission games.
  • Mermaid — shimmering, under-the-sea magic.
  • Princess — whimsical and elegant.
  • Cars & vehicles — race tracks and diggers.

For big kids (6+)

  • Superhero — bold and action-packed.
  • Spider-Man — hero training and comic-book style.
  • Dinosaur — fossil digs and adventure.
  • Winter wonderland — icy, elegant and magical.

Something completely bespoke

If your child has an idea all their own, we can build a fully bespoke theme from scratch. Browse the full range on our themes page, or tell us what they love and we'll bring it to life.

Good To Know

Common questions

How do I choose a theme my child won't outgrow by the party?
Pick something tied to a lasting interest rather than a passing craze, or keep the styling flexible — a colour-led theme like pastel rainbow works whatever mood they're in on the day.
Can you combine two themes?
Yes — we often blend ideas, like a safari-meets-rainbow setup. Tell us the two your child loves and we'll find a tasteful way to bring them together.
