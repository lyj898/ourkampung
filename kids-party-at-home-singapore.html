At Home

Parties at home, the comfiest kind of magic.

Home is often the loveliest place for a young child's party — familiar, comfortable, and easy for little ones and naps. We make it effortless: we assess your space, style it to feel genuinely special, and coordinate cake, entertainment and food, then clear everything away so your home is left exactly as we found it. All the warmth of home, none of the mess.

The Detail

How we make it effortless

  • Setups designed to fit HDB flats and condo living spaces
  • Space-smart styling that feels special without crowding the room
  • Cake, entertainment and catering coordinated together
  • Full teardown — your home left just as it was
Good To Know

At Home FAQs

Will a full setup fit in an HDB flat?
Yes — we design setups to suit the space you have, making it feel special without overwhelming the room. Share a photo of your space and we'll plan around it.
Do you clean up and remove everything afterwards?
We do — full teardown is included, and we leave your home exactly as we found it.
Can you keep noise and mess manageable for neighbours?
Of course. We plan tidy, considerate setups and can suggest quieter entertainment options for apartment living.
