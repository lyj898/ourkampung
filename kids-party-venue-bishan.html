Bishan

Kids' parties in Bishan, styled around the park life.

Bishan's biggest party asset is its greenery — Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park is right on the doorstep, and many families here live in condos like Sky Vue and Sky Habitat or HDB blocks a short walk from it. We style parties indoors in condo function rooms and flats, or bring an outdoor-ready setup if you'd rather celebrate closer to the park. Either way, we deliver, install and clear up, islandwide.

The Detail

How we make it effortless

  • Styling for Bishan condos, HDB homes and function rooms
  • Outdoor-ready setups for park-adjacent celebrations
  • Full delivery, installation and teardown in the area
  • One concierge coordinating décor, cake, entertainment and catering
Good To Know

Bishan FAQs

Can you set up near Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park?
Yes — we can style a park-adjacent celebration with a weather-safe setup, though most families still prefer the comfort of a condo function room or home with the park just a short stroll away afterwards.
Do you know the function rooms in Bishan's condos, like Sky Vue or Sky Habitat?
We regularly work in Bishan's condo function rooms and will liaise with your management on access, setup timing and house rules ahead of the day.
What's a realistic budget for a Bishan condo function room party?
Function room setups typically start from a few hundred dollars for décor and styling alone, scaling up with cake, entertainment and catering — tell us your guest count and budget and we'll tailor a package.
