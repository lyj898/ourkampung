Jurong East

Kids' parties in Jurong East, right in the west's new hub.

Jurong East has grown into one of Singapore's liveliest regional hubs — JEM, Westgate and IMM on one side, Jurong Lake Gardens and the Science Centre on the other. We style parties for families across the estate, from condos and HDB flats to function rooms near the lake district, and we deliver the full setup islandwide so distance from the east side is never a factor.

The Detail

How we make it effortless

  • Styling and setup across Jurong East condos, HDB flats and function rooms
  • Familiar with the area's newer developments around the lake district
  • Full delivery, installation and teardown, wherever you are in the west
  • Décor, cake, entertainment and catering coordinated as one plan
Good To Know

Jurong East FAQs

Do you charge more to travel to Jurong East since it's further west?
No — our styling packages include islandwide delivery and setup, so there's no extra travel surcharge for Jurong East.
Can you set up in a function room near Jurong Lake Gardens?
Yes — we regularly style function rooms across the Jurong Lake District and will coordinate directly with your venue or condo management on timing and access.
Is Jurong East good for an outdoor party, like at the lake gardens?
It can be lovely — we'll plan a weather-safe setup with shade or a wet-weather backup if you'd like to celebrate near the gardens rather than indoors.
