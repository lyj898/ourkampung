Pasir Ris

Kids' parties in Pasir Ris, a breath of park and sea air.

With Pasir Ris Park and the beach on one side and family estates and condos on the other, Pasir Ris has some of the most relaxed party settings in Singapore. We style celebrations at home, in condo function rooms, or with a park- or beach-adjacent setup if you'd like the sea breeze in the mix — delivered, installed and cleared islandwide, so the location is never a hassle for us.

The Detail

How we make it effortless

  • Styling and setup across Pasir Ris homes, condos and function rooms
  • Weather-smart, park- and beach-adjacent setups on request
  • Full delivery, installation and teardown in the area
  • Décor, cake, entertainment and catering coordinated as one plan
Good To Know

Pasir Ris FAQs

Can you set up a party at Pasir Ris Park or near the beach?
Yes — we can style a park- or beach-adjacent setup with shade and a wet-weather backup, so the sea breeze doesn't get in the way of the celebration.
Do you deliver to condos near Downtown East?
We do — we regularly style condo function rooms in that part of Pasir Ris and will coordinate with your management on access and setup timing.
Is there a surcharge for delivery to Pasir Ris?
No — islandwide delivery and setup are included in our styling packages, with no separate travel charge for Pasir Ris.
