Home/Moving house

Moving house, without the chaos.

What to book, in what order, and what catches people out on the day — for HDB flats, condos and landed homes.

Where to start

  1. Start with the week-by-week checklist. It covers the whole move, from eight weeks out to the one legal deadline afterwards. Book the things with long lead times first: movers, condo lifts and your internet line.
  2. Either home a condo? Read the move-day rules. Condos usually need a lift booking, a deposit and a set time window, and finding that out a few days before is the most common avoidable problem.
  3. Moving into a new BTO, or out of a rental? The move is the same, but there's more around it. See moving into a new home and ending a tenancy.
