Home/Moving into a new home

A new home, set up in the right order.

From key collection to the first night — inspect, renovate, clean, then move in, and don't let one step undo another.

Where to start

  1. Inspect first, so defects are on record before renovation covers them. The BTO guide covers inspection day and how the defects liability period works.
  2. Renovate, then check the work before you make the final payment. The post-renovation checklist covers what to look for, and what to do with the leftover debris.
  3. Clean once the dust has really settled, then move in — and keep noting defects, because some only show up after a few weeks.
