Our sites

OurKampung is the front door to a family of Singapore home sites, all run by one team. Here's what each one is for.

How it works

  1. One team runs every site. OurKampung and its sister sites are run by the team behind Junk to Clear, a Singapore disposal and clearance business established in 2009.
  2. The service sites match you with a partner firm. An enquiry on any of them goes to the team behind Junk to Clear, which passes it to a partner firm. The partner quotes and does the work.
  3. The guide sites answer questions. OurKampung, SwyftClear, Relocado and SpaceToReno take no bookings. They answer the questions people ask before they book, and link a service site only at the step that needs one.

Service sites

  • Service siteHomeToMovedMoving within Singapore: an HDB flat, a condo or a landed home.
  • Service siteHomeToCleanCleaning before you move in, after you move out, or after renovation.
  • Service sitePestToClearCockroaches, termites, bed bugs, rodents, ants or mosquitoes.
  • Service siteAirconToCoolAircon servicing, chemical washes, gas top-ups and repairs.
  • Service siteBrokenToFixedHandyman jobs: furniture assembly, wall mounting, doors and locks, taps, sealant and small repairs.

Guide sites

Each guide has its own lane, so the same question isn't answered on two of them.

  • Guide · you're hereOurKampungYour own home in Singapore: moving house, a new home, ending a tenancy, clearing out, repairs and upkeep.
  • GuideSwyftClearA property you're responsible for but don't live in: an inherited home, a rental between tenants, a condo you manage, business premises.
  • GuideRelocadoMoving out of Singapore, or into it: if you're getting on a plane.
  • GuideSpaceToRenoRenovating a home, from planning to the end of the works.

When it could be either

  • Ending the lease on a flat you rent? That's OurKampung's tenancy handover checklist, even if you're leaving Singapore afterwards.
  • A landlord between tenants, or clearing a relative's flat? That's SwyftClear: it covers a property you're responsible for but don't live in.
  • Clearing your own flat before you fly out? That's Relocado, which covers the whole move abroad.
  • Renovating? That's SpaceToReno, from planning to the end of the works. OurKampung keeps the BTO key-collection guide and the after-renovation checklist.
