Photography

Photography, so you're in the memories too.

The parties you host are the ones you have the fewest photos of — because you're too busy running them. We arrange relaxed, unobtrusive photographers who capture the candid moments, the details you styled, and the family portraits you'll actually treasure. You stay present with your child; we make sure the day is beautifully documented.

The Detail

What's included

  • Candid, relaxed coverage of the whole celebration
  • Detail shots of your setup, cake and styling
  • A few gentle family portraits without disrupting the party
  • Edited images delivered after the day
Good To Know

Photography FAQs

How long does the photographer stay?
Coverage typically runs one to two hours around the key moments — arrival, cake and activities. We'll advise the right length for your schedule.
When do we receive the photos?
Edited galleries are usually delivered within one to two weeks, depending on the photographer. We'll confirm timing when booking.
Can you also capture video?
We can arrange short highlight video in addition to photography — just let us know when you enquire.
