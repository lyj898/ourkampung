Paw Patrol Theme

Rescue-pup parties, adventurous and full of heart.

For the little one who's ready for action, we style rescue-pup parties full of adventure — bright primary tones, playful paw-print details and a 'rescue mission' spirit. It's energetic and instantly recognisable for the kids, and we keep the setup crisp and photogenic. From the mission games to the cake, we run the whole rescue for you.

Styling Ideas

Ways we style a paw patrol party

  • A bright balloon backdrop with playful paw-print accents
  • A 'rescue mission' obstacle course and badge ceremony
  • Pup-ear headbands or badges for every little recruit
  • A paw-print or vehicle-themed cake

Good To Know

Paw Patrol party FAQs

What age is a Paw Patrol party best for?
It's most loved by toddlers and young children, roughly ages two to six. We scale the mission games to suit the group.
Do you use official Paw Patrol characters?
We style parties inspired by the show's colours and rescue-mission theme rather than licensed artwork, so the look stays tasteful and bespoke.
Can you add entertainment or a host?
Yes — we can bring a host to run the rescue missions and games, keeping the little recruits happily occupied.
