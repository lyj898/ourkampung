Princess Theme

Princess parties, whimsical yet refined.

Every child deserves their fairytale — styled with restraint so it feels enchanting, not overwhelming. Think blush and champagne tones, tulle-draped backdrops and a castle cake worthy of the photos. Our Singapore concierge brings the whole storybook together, from the crown on arrival to the last slice of cake.

Styling Ideas

Ways we style a princess party

  • Blush and champagne balloon arch framing a tulle-draped photo nook
  • A castle or tiered cake with delicate hand-painted detail
  • Dress-up trolley with capes, crowns and wands for every guest
  • A royal tea-party table with florals and vintage-style crockery

Good To Know

Princess party FAQs

Do you provide princess dress-up and character appearances?
Yes — we can arrange dress-up trolleys for the guests and book a professional princess character to host games, storytelling and photos. Let us know which character your child loves.
Is a princess party suitable for a 1st birthday?
Absolutely. For younger celebrations we soften the theme with gentle florals and pastels, keeping it photogenic and calm for the little guest of honour.
Can you match a specific princess or film?
We can style around the colours and motifs of most popular characters while keeping the look tasteful. Tell us your child's favourite and we'll bring it to life.
