Rainbow Theme

Rainbow parties, soft, joyful, dreamy.

Rainbow doesn't have to be primary-bright. We style it in soft, sorbet pastels — a joyful gradient that feels fresh and modern. It's endlessly versatile, works for any age, and always brings a smile. Our Singapore concierge pulls together the décor, cake and details into one seamless, colourful celebration.

Styling Ideas

Ways we style a pastel rainbow party

  • A sorbet-pastel balloon arch fading through the full spectrum
  • An ombré rainbow cake with a soft buttercream finish
  • A colour-sorting play zone and rainbow party favours
  • Pastel tableware with cloud and sunshine accents

Good To Know

Pastel Rainbow party FAQs

Is a rainbow theme suitable for boys and girls?
Yes — the pastel rainbow palette is wonderfully gender-neutral and works for any age, which is part of why it's so popular.
Can you match the rainbow to specific colours I like?
Of course. If you'd prefer a warmer or cooler spectrum, or a particular accent colour, we'll tune the palette to your taste.
What sizes of party can you handle?
From intimate home gatherings to larger function-room celebrations. Tell us your guest count and we'll scale the setup to suit.
