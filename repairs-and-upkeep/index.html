Home/Repairs and upkeep

Keeping a home in good repair.

The maintenance Singapore's heat and humidity demand, and how to tell a DIY job from one that needs a professional.

Where to start

  1. Keep the aircon on a schedule. In Singapore's heat and humidity, aircons gather dust and mould fast. The aircon servicing guide explains how often to service, what each service involves and what common symptoms mean.
  2. Treat pests as year-round. Damp, food and warmth mean pests don't have a season here. The pest control guide covers what prevention handles, and when only a professional will do.
  3. Know where DIY ends. Both guides say what you can handle yourself and when to call someone. A little routine maintenance heads off most of the expensive problems.
