Home/Repairs and upkeep

Keeping a home in good repair.

The maintenance Singapore's heat and humidity demand, and how to tell a DIY job from one that needs a professional.

Singapore's heat and humidity are hard on a home. Aircons run for hours a day and gather dust and mould faster than they would in a drier climate, and damp corners, food and warmth make pests a year-round issue rather than a seasonal one. A little routine maintenance heads off most of the expensive problems.

The aircon servicing guide explains how often to service, what general servicing, chemical washes and overhauls actually involve, and what the common symptoms usually mean. The pest control guide covers the pests Singapore homes deal with most, what prevention handles, and when a professional is the only real fix.
