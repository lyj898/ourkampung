Safari & Jungle Theme

Safari parties, earthy, playful, adventurous.

A safari party is our idea of playful done tastefully — lush greenery, warm neutrals and friendly animal touches rather than loud primary colours. It's a favourite for first birthdays (hello, 'Wild One') and older adventurers alike. Our Singapore concierge styles, delivers and runs the whole thing so you can simply enjoy it.

Styling Ideas

Ways we style a safari & jungle party

  • A foliage-rich balloon installation with plush safari animals
  • A 'Wild One' cake with palm-leaf and animal-print detailing
  • An explorer craft corner — binoculars, animal masks and face painting
  • Warm neutral tableware with jute, timber and greenery accents

Good To Know

Safari & Jungle party FAQs

Is safari a good theme for a 1st birthday?
It's one of the most popular — the 'Wild One' spin is perfect for a first birthday, and the earthy palette photographs beautifully. We soften the styling for the littlest guests.
Can you add animal entertainment or mascots?
We can arrange a walkabout animal mascot, an interactive animal show, or a host to run safari-themed games, depending on your space and budget.
Do you handle setup and teardown?
Always. We deliver, set up before guests arrive, and clear everything afterwards — anywhere in Singapore.
