Space & Astronaut Theme

Space parties, blasting off in style.

A space party gives us a lot to play with — a moody navy-and-gold galaxy palette, planets suspended overhead, and just enough sparkle to feel like a proper cosmic event rather than a school science display. We style the countdown-to-cake moment as carefully as the décor, with a launch backdrop the birthday astronaut can pose in front of. Our Singapore concierge coordinates the whole mission, start to touchdown.

Styling Ideas

Ways we style a space & astronaut party

  • A galaxy balloon installation with hanging planets and a starfield backdrop
  • A rocket-ship photo nook for the birthday astronaut's launch moment
  • A 'mission control' craft table making astronaut helmets or badges
  • A planet or moon-surface cake with a metallic, cratered finish

Good To Know

Space & Astronaut party FAQs

Can you add glow or UV lighting effects for a space party?
Yes — where the venue allows, we can bring in soft glow lighting or UV accents to make the galaxy backdrop pop, especially nice for an evening celebration.
What ages does a space theme suit?
It's popular from around age three through primary school — younger guests enjoy the planets and colours, while older kids get into the mission games and badge ceremony.
Do you need a dim room for the full effect?
Not at all — the décor looks great in normal lighting too. Dimming helps if you want the glow effects, but it's entirely optional and we'll advise based on your venue.
